THESIS: The site is folded house linen: a woven linen ground, thick-thin-thick stripes woven in the host's colour, an embroidered monogram, white hemmed napkins for forms, sewn tape labels for rooms. Refuses cream-and-serif softness and the photo-hero template.

STORY: Guest meets the house name in bold marking type under a woven band, picks dates on the napkin, reads the room labels with prices, books with the total visible.
